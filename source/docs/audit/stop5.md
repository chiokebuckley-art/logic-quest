# Stop 5 · Riddle Island (Knights & Knaves): wrong-answer audit

> Since the skill-drill handoff (see `drill-stop5.md`), every lesson has guided boards before its quiz, and every hint
> shows one case already checked. Some words left some quizzes: lesson 2 has no “and” sentences, lessons 3 and 5 have
> no “us” counting words (lesson 4 teaches them), lesson 3 has no silent islanders, and lesson 5's “and” / “or”
> questions always come from a knave. Seed examples below that use those words in those lessons are from before.

Every Stop 5 item now carries the full teaching from the wrong-answer handoff (v1.0): a rule, 1–4 terms defined in place (every special word in the speech bubbles among them), what the islanders' words mean and when they are true, 2–4 worked cases, a Remember line with a question, and a smaller worked example. Each case is one full way things could be ("Vic is a knave, and Jin is a knight."), and its card gives each speaker's words as true or false, computed by `claimTrue` (or from the fact the speaker states), and says who fits the rule. Every wrong choice of every choose item has its own `ChoiceFeedback`: a headline naming that answer's gap, detail saying where it fails, and a computed counterexample case, with "Your answer: true/false" where that helps (and, for "and"/"or" choices, "The right answer: true/false", like the NOT flip's example row). The audit also reworded three vague directions (the "suppose" question, the "and"/"or" question, and the choice labels "Both" and "Neither"), and seven lesson cards now say what "both" refers to. `StopDef.fresh` now checks both sides of each idea's edge after a miss, the way the NOT flip pairs a tie with no tie.

Code: `src/engine/puzzles/knights.ts` ("teaching after a wrong answer" section, each generator, and the plan readers at the end), `src/content/stop5.ts` (lesson cards, `fresh`). Tests: `src/engine/__tests__/knights.test.ts`. They read every case card and every example back from its words, and re-compute it from the speech bubbles with a parser and an evaluator written apart from the engine. They sort each wrong choice into a kind of mistake from the cases alone and check that each kind has its own headline. Twelve mutation runs each fail the suite: flipped truths, inverted fit notes, swapped headlines, wrong examples, swapped mistake kinds, a reordered case list and a wrong smaller example. The review below added nine more (each fails it): an inverted right-answer truth, a dropped term, a counting phrase left undefined, bubble words left undefined in a guess question, the guess question's rule without the knight and knave rule, an untailored Remember line, a half-case headline, a bare "which", and a "rule out" headline.

## Terms used below

- **Case card**: a `TeachCase` such as "Uma is a knave and Vic is a knight." with "Uma’s words: false. Vic’s words: true." and a note: "Everyone fits the rule. This case works." or "Vic is a knight with false words. That breaks the rule, so this case does not work." Truths and notes are computed, never typed in.
- **Fits the rule**: a knight's words are true, or a knave's words are false. It is defined on the first lesson card and in the terms.
- **Fresh check**: the set `stop5.fresh` gives after a miss. Each set has an item like the missed one (same kind of question, same kind of answer, new names and words). Choice questions also get one on the other side of the edge. Puzzles get one more puzzle of the same size and the same kind of words.

## Shared changes suggested

All three were made after the migration. The title names who breaks the rule (“With your answer, Ivo and Mira break
the rule.”). Each line quotes the words (“If Ivo is a knave, Ivo’s words, “Mira is a knave,” must be false. With
your answer, they are true.”). The player’s own answer is shown as a card, with each speaker’s words true or false
by `claimTrue`. As first written:

- `src/engine/__tests__/stops.test.ts`: add `s5.l1` to `s5.l5` to `MIGRATED`, so the contract checks Stop 5 without `COVERAGE_ALL=1`.
- `src/engine/grade.ts`, knights puzzles: quote the speaker's words in the message ("If Vic is a knight, Vic’s words, “Uma and I are different kinds,” must be true. With your answer, they are false."). Today the player has to look back at the bubble to see which words are meant.
- `src/game/explanation.ts`, assign items with `claims`: show the player's own answer as the example card, with each speaker's words computed by `claimTrue`. For three islanders the teaching shows only the answer and the cases that change one islander, so a miss that changes two islanders has no card of its own.
